Independent Research Project

XAUUSD Historical Data & Quantitative Research

A personal technical project focused on historical market data, quantitative strategy research and backtesting tools for cTrader.

Project objectives

  • Historical market data analysis.
  • Backtesting and strategy validation.
  • Quantitative research and walk-forward evaluation.
  • Analysis of execution assumptions and trading costs.

Purpose of the application

The cTrader Open API application is intended to access authorized account information and historical market data for research and analysis. The project does not require trading permissions for historical data collection.
